## ARI VAAN (Protagonist — 5 Playable Aspects)

Ari is the same underlying person across all five realities — same core face, same core build — but each reality's version of him should read as shaped by that reality's life. Keep a consistent facial base (see "Core Face Lock" below) and vary costume/wear/posture per aspect.

### Core Face Lock (use in every Ari prompt for consistency)
`young man, mid-20s, angular but tired face, dark tousled hair, deep-set thoughtful eyes, faint scar above left eyebrow (present in all versions except Nara-0, where it hasn't happened yet), lean build, expression that reads as guarded warmth`

### Aspect 1 — The Archivist (Nara-0)
**Personality:** Quiet, observational, self-deprecating humor as a defense mechanism. The most "ordinary" version — closest to who Ari would be without any of this.
**Visual prompt:**
`young man, archivist, worn grey cardigan over simple shirt, fingerless gloves, small satchel of notes, damp from rain, modern melancholic city aesthetic, soft indoor lighting, tired eyes, [CORE FACE LOCK]`

### Aspect 2 — The Oathblade (Karth-Muun)
**Personality:** Harder, clipped speech, carries grief like armor. Protective of others to a fault.
**Visual prompt:**
`young man, battle-worn soldier, frost-covered leather and iron armor, scarred face, short practical haircut, holding a root-wood blade, frozen battlefield backdrop, grim determined expression, [CORE FACE LOCK]`

### Aspect 3 — The Patient / Machine Saint (Ora-Vell)
**Personality:** Slower, more introspective, recovering — physically and emotionally weaker in this act, which should read in posture (slight stoop, careful movement).
**Visual prompt:**
`young man, hospital-patient robes layered with golden ceremonial fabric, partial mechanical/root-light medical brace on one arm, pale, exhausted but alert eyes, opulent golden hospital backdrop, [CORE FACE LOCK]`

### Aspect 4 — Glass Eden Ari (no formal title — closest to "true self")
**Personality:** The least guarded version. Softer voice, more open expressions — this is what Ari looks/sounds like without a role to hide behind.
**Visual prompt:**
`young man, simple pale clothing, no armor or props, standing in an unnervingly perfect golden garden, soft directionless light, calm but subtly uneasy expression, [CORE FACE LOCK]`

### Aspect 5 — Root Below / Composite Ari (Acts VIII–X)
**Personality:** All aspects converging — should read as weary synthesis, not a new persona.
**Visual prompt:**
`young man, faint overlapping translucent echoes of four other versions of himself layered behind his silhouette, standing in dead grey root-matter graveyard, cracked root-light veins faintly visible under his skin, solemn, [CORE FACE LOCK]`

## MIRAEL

**Role:** Ari's closest friend across every reality; the emotional axis of the story. NOT a prize, NOT cruel — written and must be rendered as a full person with her own steadiness.

**Personality:** Warm, direct, unafraid of hard conversations. Never leads Ari on. Her care for him is real and stated plainly, which is part of why the story's ache lands — there's no ambiguity to hide behind.

**Visual prompt (base):**
`young woman, mid-20s, warm brown eyes, dark hair often tied back practically, calm expressive face, dresses plainly and comfortably rather than decoratively, carries herself with quiet confidence, [apply reality-appropriate clothing per act — rain-city casual for Nara-0, practical field clothing for Karth-Muun, soft flowing pale fabric for Glass Eden]`

**Key visual note:** Do NOT render her as conventionally "prize"-coded (no idealized/objectifying framing). Her design should emphasize warmth and steadiness over glamour.

---

## KAEL ORIN

**Role:** The person Mirael chooses, in the realities where a choice like that exists. A genuinely good person — never smug, never a rival in the antagonistic sense.

**Personality:** Steady, plainspoken, kind without performing kindness. Has his own grief (war, loss) that Ari never fully sees, since Ari's pain crowds out the space to notice.

**Visual prompt:**
`young man, early-to-mid 20s, broad steady build, short practical hair, faint old scar near jaw, calm grounded expression, dressed as a soldier/field-worker in Karth-Muun, weathered but gentle face, warm brown eyes, non-threatening posture`

---

## VEYR SOL AURIC

**Role:** The primary antagonist. A grieving father whose logic is coherent, whose pain is real, and who is never played as simply "evil."

**Personality:** Calm, articulate, almost gentle in conversation — his menace comes from conviction, not cruelty. Speaks slowly, like every sentence has been rehearsed alone for years.

**Visual prompt:**
`older man, late 40s to 50s, tall, gaunt from years of grief and obsession, once-fine golden robes now worn and root-scarred, long dark hair streaked grey, hollow but intense eyes, carries the Nullroot [see weapons doc] wrapped in cloth like something sacred, Ora-Vell golden-hospital origin aesthetic bleeding into darker root-black accents, dignified rather than monstrous, deep melancholic expression`

**Key visual note:** He should never look feral or inhuman. The horror of Veyr is that he looks like someone's father — because he is.

---

## SENN

**Role:** The herald/avatar of the Witness Mind — warm, funny, protective, and ultimately the story's quiet betrayer-by-omission.

**Personality:** Dry humor, old-soul warmth, genuinely affectionate toward Ari — the betrayal lands harder because the affection is never fake. Speaks in short, precise sentences; avoids long explanations (a character trait that becomes sinister in hindsight).

**Visual prompt:**
`small floating creature, half root/plant half spirit-light, roughly cat-to-fox sized, humanoid-suggestive but not humanoid, bioluminescent veins under bark-like skin, gentle expressive "face" formed from light patterns rather than fixed features, warm teal-gold glow, ancient but small, non-threatening silhouette, faint particle trail when it moves`

---

## THE WITNESS MIND

**Role:** The vast, dying consciousness rooted in the Heartwood. Only fully "seen" in Act X.

**Personality:** Not cruel, not warm in the way Senn is — vast, patient, grieving in a register too large for individual words. When it finally speaks directly, it should not sound apologetic in a human way; it sounds like something ancient reckoning honestly, without performing remorse.

**Visual prompt:**
`vast cosmic tree-root structure filling the entire frame, humanoid face barely suggested within the root patterns — visible only if you look for it, glowing faintly from deep within countless branching veins, scale meant to feel incomprehensibly large next to a human figure, dying light flickering at damaged sections, awe and sorrow combined, dark void background`

## Voice Casting Notes Summary

| Character | Voice Direction |
|---|---|
| Ari (all aspects) | Same actor, subtle register shifts per aspect — quieter/younger in Nara-0, harder/clipped in Karth-Muun, weaker/breathier in Ora-Vell |

| Witness Mind | Deep, vast, deliberately under-emoted — let the vastness read as restraint, not coldness |
