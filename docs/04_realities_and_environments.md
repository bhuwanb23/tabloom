# TABLOOM: The Last Mind of Aevum
### Document 4 of 5 — Realities & Environments (Full Asset-Generation Descriptions)

Each reality needs: an overall mood/palette identity (so a player instantly knows which reality they're in from color alone), 2-4 key locations, and generation prompts for environment art, skyboxes, and key set-pieces.

---

## NARA-0 — The Rain City

**Mood identity:** Melancholic, modern, grey-blue palette with warm indoor amber accents. Rain is a constant environmental character, not just weather.

**Key locations:**
1. **Ari's Apartment** (opening scene, memory-lock puzzle)
2. **The Rain Streets** (connective/exploration space)
3. **The Archive** (Ari's workplace — ties to his "Archivist" title, houses lore/collectible items)

**Environment prompts:**
```
Apartment interior: small modern apartment interior, rain streaking down
a large window, dim warm lamp light contrasting cold blue rain-light,
worn but lived-in furniture, papers and notes scattered on a desk,
melancholic atmosphere, dark fantasy realism, no text, no watermark

Rain streets: narrow rain-soaked city street at night, neon-adjacent but
muted signage, reflections pooling on wet asphalt, lonely atmosphere,
figures indistinct in the distance, moody blue-grey palette with warm
window-light accents, cinematic wide shot, no text, no watermark

The Archive: vast quiet library/archive interior, towering shelves
disappearing into shadow above, single figure dwarfed by scale, dust
motes in narrow light beams, muted warm-grey palette, contemplative
atmosphere, no text, no watermark
```

## KARTH-MUUN — The Frozen War

**Mood identity:** Cold, harsh, high-contrast — pale ice-white and gunmetal grey, with the Frost-Curse Heart's red-black glow as the only "wrong" color in the palette.

**Key locations:**
1. **The Frozen Battlefield** (main combat arena, loop-locked)
2. **The Ice Gate** (connects narratively to Nara-0's lamp-puzzle ripple)
3. **The Curse-Heart Chamber** (boss location, Acts II & VII)

**Environment prompts:**
```
Frozen battlefield: vast frozen battlefield frozen mid-chaos, soldiers
and banners caught in unnatural stillness, ice crystals suspended
mid-air as if time stopped, pale cold light, gunmetal and white palette,
epic scale, dark fantasy war aesthetic, no text, no watermark

Ice gate: massive ancient gate half-sealed in thick ice, faint warm
light bleeding through a crack where it has begun to melt, imposing
scale, cold blue-white lighting, fantasy architecture, no text,
no watermark

Curse-heart chamber: dark ice cavern chamber, pulsing crystalline
heart-object embedded in the center, red-black cracks of light spreading
through the ice around it, oppressive claustrophobic scale, dramatic
low lighting, no text, no watermark
```

## ORA-VELL — The Golden Hospital-Empire

**Mood identity:** Opulent but sick — gold and cream architecture with an undercurrent of wrongness (slightly too-perfect symmetry, sterile excess). Warmest palette of all five realities, deliberately so, to contrast the coldest story content.

**Key locations:**
1. **The Grand Ward** (main exploration hub)
2. **Veyr's Private Archive** (the document/timeline puzzle room)
3. **The Ritual Operating Theatre** (where the Nullroot's origin is implied, never fully shown)

**Environment prompts:**
```
Grand Ward: vast opulent hospital hall, golden columns and marble
floors, soft diffuse light from high arched windows, rows of ornate
medical beds mostly empty, unsettling cleanliness, warm golden palette
with a sterile undertone, dark fantasy opulence, no text, no watermark

Veyr's archive: private study room filled with scattered medical logs
and hanging charts, golden light through narrow windows, a single
child's drawing pinned incongruously above a workstation, intimate and
sorrowful scale, warm but melancholic lighting, no text, no watermark

Ritual theatre: circular ceremonial medical theatre, golden fixtures,
strange root-light medical apparatus, empty operating table at center,
reverent and unsettling atmosphere, dramatic overhead lighting, no text,
no watermark
```

## GLASS EDEN — The False Paradise

**Mood identity:** Too-perfect. Golden-white light with no true shadows, unnaturally saturated greenery, a palette that should feel *wrong* the longer the player looks at it — slightly oversaturated compared to every other reality.

**Key locations:**
1. **The Eternal Garden** (main space, the almost-love scene)
2. **The Mirror Pool** (the "find the flaw" puzzle location)

**Environment prompts:**
```
Eternal garden: impossibly perfect golden garden, flowers that never
wilt, soft warm directionless light with no visible sun and no true
shadows, unnervingly symmetrical landscaping, dreamlike oversaturated
palette, subtle uncanny quality, no text, no watermark

Mirror pool: still perfectly reflective pool at the garden's center,
reflecting a sky that doesn't quite match the real one above it, soft
golden light, quiet unsettling beauty, dark fantasy dreamscape, no text,
no watermark
```

---

## THE ROOT BELOW — The Graveyard of Dead Timelines

**Mood identity:** The starkest palette shift in the game — desaturated almost to greyscale, with only faint dying embers of color remaining in the decayed root-matter. This should feel like walking through a photograph left in the rain.

**Key locations:**
1. **The Ten Dead Branches** (main exploration space)
2. **The Dead Ari Site** (Acts III vision & VIII full reveal — needs two versions, see below)

**Environment prompts:**
```
Dead branches: graveyard of massive blackened tree-branches, root-light
extinguished to grey ash, decayed and lifeless, floating in dark void,
faint embers still falling like snow, mournful desaturated palette,
epic somber scale, dark fantasy, no text, no watermark

Dead Ari site (Act III, vision/unstable version): same graveyard
environment but with subtle visual distortion — double-exposure ghosting
effect, colors bleeding slightly, dreamlike instability, unsettling,
no text, no watermark

Dead Ari site (Act VIII, full/stable version): same location fully
rendered and stable, clear enough to read carved text at the base of
the dead figure, grey-black root-matter grown through a humanoid form,
mournful clarity, no text, no watermark
```

---

## THE HEARTWOOD — The Root of Everything

**Mood identity:** The game's final and most sacred space — should feel larger than every other environment combined, and should visually carry scars from the Shattering (the crack introduced in the intro cinematic) as a permanent environmental feature.

**Key locations:**
1. **The Siege Approach** (Act IX arena, boss fight with Veyr)
2. **The Root Chamber** (Act X, the Witness Mind's true form, the three-ending choice space)

**Environment prompts:**
```
Siege approach: vast root-bridge suspended over an incomprehensible dark
void, ancient massive tree-structure surrounding on all sides, glowing
wounded crack running through the central trunk in the distance, epic
battle-arena scale, dramatic teal-gold lighting against deep void black,
no text, no watermark

Root chamber: innermost chamber of the great tree, a vast dying but
still glowing root-mass filling the space, faint humanoid face barely
suggested within the countless branching veins, awe-inspiring and
sorrowful scale, dark void background, single dramatic light source,
no text, no watermark
```

|---|---|---|
| Nara-0 | Grey-blue | Warm amber |
| Karth-Muun | Pale ice-white / gunmetal | Red-black (curse) |
| Ora-Vell | Warm gold / cream | Sterile white |
| Glass Eden | Oversaturated gold-white | Unnatural green |
| The Root Below | Desaturated grey | Dying ember-orange |
| The Heartwood | Deep void-black | Teal-gold (living) / grey-white (wound) |

**Use case:** each reality's browser-tab icon (core UI element for the tab-switch mechanic) should be color-coded per this table so players can identify realities at a glance without reading labels.
