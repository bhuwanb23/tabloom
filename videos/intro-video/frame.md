## Concept angle

Aevum as a living World-Tree of Becoming: Remembrance light against void, grief as auric gold, Bleed as black-gold fracture. Motion-comic stills, not anime loops.

## Motion language

- Myth beats: slow push-in (scale 1.0 → 1.08), parallax layers ±20–40px
- Grief: gold light bloom + soft drift
- Shattering: crack reveal + brief screen shake (finite)
- Intimate Ari: shallower depth, rain overlay
- Branchfall: glitch RGB-split, hard cut
- Title: slam hold 2.5s+
