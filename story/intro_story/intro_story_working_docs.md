Below is the short, precise production plan for creating the intro video so it feels premium even though the game is 2D. The goal is not full animation, but a high-end 2D motion-cinematic intro with strong visuals, narrator voice, music, and mythic pacing.

Intro Video Style

Use a 2D motion comic cinematic.

That means:

Static painted images
Layered parallax movement
Slow camera zooms and pans
Rain, particles, smoke, glowing cracks
Text overlays
Strong narrator voice
Minimal character animation
Powerful music and sound design

This is best for a 2D game jam because it looks professional without needing full animation.

Intro Duration

Keep it around:

90 seconds to 120 seconds


Do not make it too long.

The player should feel:

“This world is huge, mysterious, emotional, and dangerous.”

Video Structure
1. Myth of Aevum

Show the cosmic tree and realities.

Visuals needed:

Aevum tree in dark void
15 glowing branches
small glimpses of different worlds
white and gold memory particles

Narrator explains:

Aevum was the tree of realities.
 The Mind kept worlds apart.

2. The Mind

Show the ancient consciousness.

Visuals needed:

faceless glowing humanoid figure
many silhouettes inside one body
floating memory threads
Veil of Severance as a glowing barrier

Narrator explains:

The Mind did not rule. It remembered.

3. Veyr and the Lament Engine

Show villain origin and how he defeated the Mind.

Visuals needed:

Veyr in golden hospital world
Luma on a medical bed
Veyr seeing another version of Luma alive
Concord of One masks
Lament Engine cathedral
golden-black grief energy

Narrator explains:

Veyr did not defeat the Mind by force.
 He drowned it in grief.

4. The Shattering

Show reality breaking.

Visuals needed:

Aevum cracking
worlds bleeding into each other
rain turning into snow
hospital bed on battlefield
roots growing through city
black-gold cracks

Narrator explains:

The Veil failed.
 The boughs began to bleed.

5. Ari Wakes

Show the protagonist.

Visuals needed:

Nara-0 apartment
rain window
Ari on floor
terminal
broken mirror
15 shadows on wall

Narrator explains:

The Mind sent one splinter into Ari Vaan.

6. Mirael Hope

Do not reveal the truth.

Visuals needed:

Mirael as childhood friend
Ari and Mirael in rain
Kael standing gently in background
Ari reaching toward a memory
Mirael’s face partly hidden or blurred

Narrator says:

In his heart remained a name: Mirael.
 And where hope still lived, the Mind placed a key.

Important:
 Do not say she never loves him. Keep that for the final reveal.

7. The First Branch Opens

Show the browser-tab concept.

Visuals needed:

terminal text
first tab glitch
second tab opens
Karth-Muun battlefield
Ari the Oathblade waking in snow

Final line:

“I remember dying.”

Then title:

TABLOOM: THE LAST MIND OF AEVUM

Final Recommendation

Make the intro as a 2D mythic motion cinematic, not full animation. Use strong painted stills, parallax, narrator voice, rain, particles, glitch effects, and powerful sound. The intro should hide the final truth and create hope around Mirael, while establishing Aevum, the Mind, Veyr, the Lament Engine, the Shattering, and Ari’s first awakening.

Main Improvement Summary

This plan keeps the intro achievable while making it feel cinematic. The focus is on strong art direction, layered motion, voice narration, and emotional mystery instead of expensive animation.
